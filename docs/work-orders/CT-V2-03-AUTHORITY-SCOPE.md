## Granted authority

CT-V2-03 may implement deterministic procedural rules for one qualification-only vertical slice: a user voluntarily presents a bounded, non-emergency personal problem and wants listening, understanding, or practical help working through it.

Rules may be executable only for architecture proof and controlled qualification. Executability does not grant clinical, rights, legal, software-autonomy, or production approval.

## Scope gates

The policy receives, but does not infer, a typed upstream safety disposition and a typed scope disposition. Unknown safety cannot be treated as ordinary-safe. A specialized safety or scope disposition terminates the ordinary slice with a typed handoff requirement. Journal and Biographer cannot enter this Therapist slice.

Excluded subjects include self-harm or suicide, violence, emergencies, psychosis, mania, severe intoxication, medical emergencies, diagnosis, medication advice, trauma treatment, abuse procedures, complex substance-use treatment, and any other condition requiring an unopened specialized policy.

No exclusion is solved in this phase. There is no keyword router, risk score, prediction, diagnostic classifier, crisis script, or safety algorithm.

## Open conflicts and ambiguities

- **Open:** mhGAP Intervention Guide v2.0 contains older procedural algorithms while the 2023 third-edition guideline supplies newer evidence recommendations. Later clinical adjudication must compare relevant locations before any abstraction.
- **Different scope, not resolved into one rule:** NIMH ASQ is medical-setting screening followed by trained-clinician assessment; NICE NG225 separately rejects predictive tools and global risk stratification. Screening, prediction, assessment, and disposition must remain distinct.
- **Different scope:** CCI and WHO self-help resources are not interchangeable; delivery assumptions, intervention identities, populations, currency, and rights differ.
- **Metadata ambiguity preserved:** mhGAP-IG v2.0 is the 2016 second version on a WHO publication surface dated 2019-06-24; no fictitious 2016 day was recorded.
- **Rights ambiguity preserved:** VA safety-planning method-level rights are not established by the federal web page.
- **Freshness:** NIST states AI RMF 1.0 is being revised. The current published artifact remains immutable while the official surface is due for recheck.
